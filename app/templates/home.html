About LAKSHYA

LAKSHYA is a student productivity platform that turns studying into a game. It pairs task tracking, XP, levels, and streaks with an AI assistant, so students stay consistent and get help when they are stuck. Built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript as a college project.
